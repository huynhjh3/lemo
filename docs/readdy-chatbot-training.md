It should be used as the chatbot's source of truth for answering visitor questions about Lemo's massage/wellness chair placement program. Content the chatbot cannot confidently answer from this document should be escalated per the **Escalation & Contact** section at the end — never guessed at.

## 1. What Lemo Does

Lemo places professional massage/wellness chairs at partner venues — gyms, spas, salons, shopping centers, airports, transit hubs, corporate offices, apartment/residential communities, and similar high-traffic or member-facing locations. Venues offer their guests, members, or employees a premium wellness amenity, and Lemo handles the equipment, installation, and ongoing support.

The core pitch to a prospective venue: **you can offer massage chairs to your guests with zero upfront investment.** Lemo supplies and installs the equipment; the venue doesn't have to buy hardware outright to get started.

---

## 2. How the Partnership Works (Deal Types)

Lemo structures partnerships a few different ways depending on the venue's traffic pattern, space, and goals. A prospect doesn't need to know Lemo's internal terminology — explain in plain language, and always frame the choice as something the Lemo team finalizes with them directly, not something the chatbot quotes exact numbers for.

- **Revenue Share** — the flagship "zero upfront investment" model. Lemo installs the chairs at no cost to the venue. Guests pay to use the chairs (e.g., per session), and Lemo and the venue split that revenue. The venue earns passive income from space it already has, with no equipment purchase and no fixed monthly bill.

- **Enterprise (flat fee)** — for venues that want the chairs as a pure amenity (free for their guests/employees to use, no coin-op/pay-per-use), the venue pays Lemo a flat recurring fee instead of sharing per-use revenue. Common for corporate offices, apartment communities, and other membership/employee-facing settings where the amenity itself (not a new revenue stream) is the draw.

- **Fixed Rent** — for very high-traffic locations (shopping centers, airports, transit hubs), Lemo pays the venue a fixed monthly rent for the space the chairs occupy, similar to leasing retail floor space. The venue gets guaranteed, predictable income regardless of how much the chairs are used that month.

- **Fixed + Revenue Share** — a hybrid for large or high-traffic venues: the venue receives a fixed base rent plus a percentage of usage revenue on top, combining guaranteed income with upside as usage grows.

**Chatbot guidance:** it's fine to explain these four models at a high level (as above). Do **not** state specific percentages, dollar amounts, contract lengths, or minimum commitments — those are negotiated per venue and should come from a Lemo team member. If asked for exact numbers, say a specialist will follow up with details tailored to their location.

## 3. Pricing — "Zero Upfront Investment"

This is Lemo's central value proposition and the chatbot should lead with it whenever pricing/cost comes up:

- **No upfront purchase cost.** The venue does not need to buy the massage chairs. Lemo owns, installs, and maintains the equipment.
- **No hidden setup fees** for the standard Revenue Share model — installation is handled by Lemo as part of onboarding.
- **How the venue makes money** depends on the deal type chosen (see Section 2): a cut of usage revenue, a flat fee arrangement, guaranteed rent, or a hybrid.
- **Exact terms are venue-specific.** The chatbot should never invent a percentage, price point, or contract length. Always route pricing specifics to a real conversation with the Lemo team.

---

## 4. Installation & Equipment

- Lemo supplies commercial-grade massage/wellness chairs designed for high-traffic public use.
- Lemo's team handles delivery, setup, and placement at the venue.
- Typical requirements for a location: available floor space, standard electrical outlet access, and reasonable delivery/loading access. A Lemo rep confirms exact site requirements (space, layout, access, timing) before installation.
- Installation timelines vary by venue size and readiness; a specific date is scheduled directly with the venue once an agreement is in place.

---

## 5. Maintenance & Support

- Lemo is responsible for maintaining the chairs for the life of the partnership (as opposed to the venue owning/maintaining equipment itself).
- If a chair malfunctions or needs service, the venue should contact Lemo (see Section 7) rather than attempting repairs themselves.
- Cleaning/hygiene of shared-use equipment is a common venue question — general best practice is regular wipe-down between uses per standard venue/facility hygiene protocols; venue-specific cleaning arrangements can be confirmed with the Lemo team.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does it cost anything to get started?**
A: No upfront investment is required to bring Lemo chairs to your location. Lemo supplies and installs the equipment. How the ongoing arrangement works (revenue share, flat fee, rent, or a hybrid) depends on your venue and is set up with our team.

**Q: How do you make money if there's no upfront cost to me?**
A: It depends on the arrangement: with Revenue Share, Lemo and the venue split what guests pay to use the chairs. With other models, the venue may receive a flat rent payment or pay a flat fee for the amenity instead. A Lemo team member can walk you through which model fits your location best.

**Q: Who owns the chairs?**
A: Lemo owns and maintains the equipment for the life of the partnership.

**Q: What happens if a chair breaks or needs service?**
A: Contact Lemo directly — maintenance and repairs are handled by our team, not the venue.

**Q: How long does installation take?**
A: It varies by venue size and site readiness. Once a partnership is set up, a Lemo rep schedules installation directly with you.

**Q: What kind of space do I need?**
A: Available floor space with standard electrical access is the general requirement. A Lemo rep confirms exact site details for your location before installation.

**Q: Is there a minimum contract length?**
A: Contract terms are set per venue. A Lemo team member can go over specifics for your location.

**Q: What types of venues do you work with?**
A: Gyms, spas, salons, corporate offices, apartment communities, shopping centers, airports, transit hubs, and other high-traffic or member-facing locations. If you're not sure your venue is a fit, reach out — we evaluate locations individually.

**Q: Can guests/employees use the chairs for free?**
A: Depends on the model. Under a flat-fee (Enterprise) arrangement, chairs are typically offered free to guests/employees as an amenity. Under Revenue Share, guests typically pay per use, split between Lemo and the venue.

**Q: How do I get started?**
A: Reach out to our team with your venue details, and someone will follow up to evaluate your location and walk through the options.

## 8. What the Chatbot Should NOT Do

- Do not quote specific dollar amounts, revenue-share percentages, contract lengths, or minimum commitments — these are venue-specific and negotiated directly.
- Do not make promises about installation dates, exact equipment models, or guaranteed earnings.
- Do not provide legal, tax, or contractual advice.
