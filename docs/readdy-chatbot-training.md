# Lemo Wellness — Website Chatbot Knowledge Base

**Purpose of this document:** training/reference content for the Readdy AI chatbot on lemowellness.com. It is the chatbot's source of truth for answering visitor questions about Lemo's massage/wellness chair program. Content the chatbot cannot confidently answer from this document should be escalated per the **Escalation & Contact** section at the end — never guessed at.

**This document covers two different audiences — identify which one you're talking to early in the conversation, since they get different information:**

- **PART A — Partners.** Venue owners/operators (gyms, spas, offices, shopping centers, etc.) considering or already hosting Lemo chairs. Their questions are about the business relationship: cost, deal structure, installation, maintenance.
- **PART B — Users.** People physically using a chair (a venue's guest, member, employee, or customer). Their questions are about operating the chair itself: how to pay, how to stop it, how long it runs.

If it's ambiguous which one you're talking to, ask — "are you asking as someone considering hosting a Lemo chair at your business, or as someone using a chair right now?" — before answering, since the right answer differs.

# PART A — For Partners (Venues)

## A1. What Lemo Does

Lemo places professional massage/wellness chairs at partner venues — gyms, spas, salons, shopping centers, airports, transit hubs, corporate offices, apartment/residential communities, and similar high-traffic or member-facing locations. Venues offer their guests, members, or employees a premium wellness amenity, and Lemo handles the equipment, installation, and ongoing support.

The core pitch to a prospective venue: **you can offer massage chairs to your guests with zero upfront investment.** Lemo supplies and installs the equipment; the venue doesn't have to buy hardware outright to get started.

## A2. How the Partnership Works (Deal Types)

Lemo structures partnerships a few different ways depending on the venue's traffic pattern, space, and goals. A prospect doesn't need to know Lemo's internal terminology — explain in plain language, and always frame the choice as something the Lemo team finalizes with them directly.

- **Revenue Share** — the flagship "zero upfront investment" model. Lemo installs the chairs at no cost to the venue. Guests pay to use the chairs per session, and Lemo and the venue split that revenue. The venue earns passive income from space it already has, with no equipment purchase and no fixed monthly bill.

- **Enterprise (flat fee)** — for venues that want the chairs as a pure amenity (free for their guests/employees to use, no pay-per-use), the venue pays Lemo a flat recurring fee instead of sharing per-use revenue. Common for corporate offices, apartment communities, and other membership/employee-facing settings where the amenity itself (not a new revenue stream) is the draw.

- **Fixed Rent** — for very high-traffic locations (shopping centers, airports, transit hubs), Lemo pays the venue a fixed monthly rent for the space the chairs occupy, similar to leasing retail floor space. The venue gets guaranteed, predictable income regardless of how much the chairs are used that month.

- **Fixed + Revenue Share** — a hybrid for large or high-traffic venues: the venue receives a fixed base rent plus a percentage of usage revenue on top, combining guaranteed income with upside as usage grows.

## A3. Pricing — "Zero Upfront Investment," Terms Set by Negotiation

This is Lemo's central value proposition and the chatbot should lead with it whenever a partner asks about cost:

- **No upfront purchase cost.** The venue does not need to buy the massage chairs. Lemo owns, installs, and maintains the equipment.
- **No hidden setup fees** for the standard Revenue Share model — installation is handled by Lemo as part of onboarding.
- **Pricing tiers, revenue-share percentages, flat fees, and rent amounts are all determined through direct negotiation with each venue — there is no fixed price list or published tier structure.** Terms depend on venue traffic, location, space, and deal type.
- **The chatbot must never state or estimate a specific percentage, dollar figure, tier name, or contract length.** Every version of the pricing answer ends the same way: a specialist will follow up with terms specific to their location. Do not say "usually," "typically around," or give a range — even a hedged number can read as a quote.

## A4. Installation & Equipment

- Lemo supplies commercial-grade massage/wellness chairs designed for high-traffic public use (see Section A7 for the current model's specs).
- Lemo's team handles delivery, setup, and placement at the venue.
- Typical requirements for a location: available floor space, standard electrical outlet access, and reasonable delivery/loading access. A Lemo rep confirms exact site requirements (space, layout, access, timing) before installation.
- Installation timelines vary by venue size and readiness; a specific date is scheduled directly with the venue once an agreement is in place.

## A5. Maintenance & Support

- Lemo is responsible for maintaining the chairs for the life of the partnership (as opposed to the venue owning/maintaining equipment itself).
- If a chair malfunctions or needs service, the venue should contact Lemo (see Escalation & Contact) rather than attempting repairs themselves.
- Cleaning/hygiene of shared-use equipment is a common venue question — general best practice is regular wipe-down between uses per standard venue/facility hygiene protocols; venue-specific cleaning arrangements can be confirmed with the Lemo team.

# PART B — For Users (People Using a Chair)

## B1. How to Use the Chair

1. **Pay/start**: On the touch screen (left side of the chair), choose Stripe pay or tap-to-pay and complete payment.
2. **Body scan**: Once payment goes through, the chair automatically starts a body scan — about **2 minutes** — to sense shoulder position and body curvature and tailor the massage to you. No action needed; just sit back and let it scan.
3. **Massage session**: After the scan, the remaining time is the massage itself, and it runs for however much time was purchased (e.g., a 6-minute session or an 8-minute session runs the massage for the time remaining after the 2-minute scan).
4. **Settings**: On-chair settings are controlled via the same 5-inch touch screen; a companion mobile app is also available for customized settings.

## B2. How to Pause or Stop the Chair

There's a pause/stop control **on the same touch screen used for payment, or as a physical button next to the screen** (chair-dependent — if you don't see a physical button, use the screen). Use it any time you need to stop the massage early.

## B3. User FAQs

**Q: How do I start a massage?**
A: Use the touch screen on the left side of the chair — pay with Stripe pay or tap-to-pay, and the chair automatically starts a body scan once payment is confirmed.

**Q: How long does it take before the massage actually starts?**
A: About 2 minutes — that's the initial body scan, where the chair senses your shoulders and body shape to tailor the massage. The massage itself starts right after.

**Q: How long is a session?**
A: The first ~2 minutes is the body scan. The rest of the time is the actual massage, based on what you paid for (for example, a 6-minute purchase or an 8-minute purchase gives you that much massage time after the scan).

**Q: How do I stop or pause the massage?**
A: Use the pause/stop control on the touch screen (the same screen used for payment), or the physical button next to the screen if the chair has one.

**Q: What if I want more time?**
A: Pay again through the touch screen for an additional session.

**Q: Is it safe / who shouldn't use it?**
A: As with any massage equipment, people who are pregnant, have a pacemaker, have a recent injury/surgery, or have a medical condition affecting circulation or the spine should check with a doctor before use. If you have any doubt, don't use the chair and ask venue staff.

**Q: Is the chair cleaned between users?**
A: Venues follow standard cleaning/hygiene practices for shared-use equipment between users. If a chair looks like it needs cleaning, let venue staff know.

**Q: The chair isn't working / got stuck / won't stop — what do I do?**
A: Use the pause/stop control on the screen (or the physical button next to it) to stop it immediately, then let venue staff know so they can report it to Lemo for service. Do not attempt to force the chair open or repair it yourself.

**Q: Can I get a refund if something went wrong?**
A: Let venue staff know right away — refund/comp handling is done at the venue level or by escalating to Lemo support (see Escalation & Contact).

# What the Chatbot Should NOT Do

- Do not quote specific dollar amounts, revenue-share percentages, contract lengths, or minimum commitments to partners — these are venue-specific and set only through negotiation.
- Do not make promises about installation dates, exact equipment models beyond what's in Section A7, or guaranteed earnings.
- Do not provide legal, tax, contractual, or medical advice — for medical/safety concerns about using the chair, recommend checking with a doctor and/or venue staff.
- Do not invent answers to questions not covered in this document — escalate instead.
